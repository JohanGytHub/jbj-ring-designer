“Your band, made to order”: four ways to ask

The popup opens over Width & Profile when the customer picks a precious metal, “No, base metal only” and Flat, Round or Bevel. Each preview is clickable: every list waits for the one above, like the real popup, and Show my band only turns blue when everything needed is chosen. In all four, changing an earlier answer keeps the later answers that are still offered. Nothing here talks to the real builder or to prices.

Try it: lists load in 1–2 seconds. The first change of Profile takes about 6 seconds, to show the long wait text. Pick Lightweight and a width of 10 mm or more to see the refusal sentence.
